The public bargain

Five rules ordinary people can audit.

The package is intentionally smaller than the technology. It uses existing institutions, establishes hard worker protections, and publishes whether the investment worked.

  1. RULE 01

    Workers come before technology.

    Public funds cannot finance replacement layoffs, worker misclassification, or unpaid productive work disguised as training.

  2. RULE 02

    Skills must cross into real work.

    Advanced training must produce externally evaluated evidence in paid work, apprenticeship, research, public service, or genuine community use.

  3. RULE 03

    The worker owns the record.

    No centralized federal AI score. Portable learning and employment records remain voluntary, selective, and controlled by the individual.

  4. RULE 04

    Use what already exists.

    Workforce Pell, WIOA, Registered Apprenticeship, state systems, job centers, colleges, libraries, unions, and tribal institutions do the work.

  5. RULE 05

    Prove it or stop it.

    New authority sunsets after five years. Continuation requires public outcomes, independent evaluation, and another vote.

The package

Four connected functions. No parallel federal machine.

Each title has one job: measure change, protect workers, fund paid transitions, and verify outcomes without building a national person-level database.

TITLE I / MEASURE

Know where work is changing.

Combine labor-market evidence, employer notices, and public reporting so interventions follow actual displacement risk.

Aggregate data · no person-level ledger
TITLE II / PROTECT

Attach conditions to public money.

No-displacement, wage, classification, privacy, and paid-training rules follow every participating employer and provider.

Clawbacks · ineligibility · public violations
TITLE III / PATHWAYS

Pay people to learn through work.

Use Workforce Pell, WIOA support, and Registered Apprenticeship for paid pathways into occupations being redesigned by AI.

States and local institutions deliver
TITLE IV / PROOF

Verify skills and publish results.

Workers carry selective evidence. The public sees placement, earnings, retention, cost, complaints, and failures in aggregate.

Five-year cap · no automatic renewal
A human pathway

AI access is not the outcome.

The public objective is verified capability applied to consequential work. A person should leave with better leverage in the world—not merely more generated material.

  1. 01Find a paid pathway through an existing local institution.
  2. 02Learn on real problems with another person’s standards and consequences.
  3. 03Keep portable evidence and disclose only what the worker chooses.
  4. 04Measure the outcome six and twelve months later.
A related working demonstration

Here is AI used as a narrow instrument—not an authority.

The Public Service Router helps someone describe a government task in ordinary language and reach a reviewed official source. It does not answer the government question, decide eligibility, or invent a URL.

PUBLIC SERVICE ROUTER / U.S.EXPERIMENTAL

Do not enter a Social Security number, case number, medical details, password, or other sensitive identifier. Your text is sent to the configured model provider for one constrained verification call.

TRY:
Describe the task, not the agency.

The system searches reviewed records, asks a small model to verify one candidate or abstain, and shows a disclosure before you leave.

WHY THIS BELONGS HERE

The tool is the policy principle in software.

  1. 1Emergency patterns bypass the model.
  2. 2Reviewed records are retrieved locally.
  3. 3The model may verify one record or abstain.
  4. 4Invalid or low-confidence output is rejected.
  5. 5Only human-authored content and URLs render.
A confident wrong route is the primary failure.
“I’m not sure” is a designed outcome, not an error.

Hostile-hearing answers

The obvious objections, answered directly.

Is this an AI acceleration bill?+

No. It is a limited worker-transition demonstration. It does not mandate AI adoption or fund model development.

Does it create a federal database of every worker’s skills?+

No. A centralized federal person-level Skills Ledger is prohibited. Portable records remain voluntary and worker-controlled.

Can a company use the money to fire people?+

No. No-displacement, wage, classification, and paid-training rules apply, with clawbacks and future ineligibility for misuse.

Is the service router an official government assistant?+

No. It is an experimental public directory. It routes only to reviewed sources, makes no eligibility decision, and abstains when uncertain.

What happens when the demonstration fails?+

New authority ends after five years. Congress receives results, costs, complaints, and violations, and must pass a new law to continue.

FINAL PROPOSITION / AI WORKS

Protect the worker. Fund the pathway. Verify the skill. Let the worker own the record. Publish the result. Stop the program if it does not work.
